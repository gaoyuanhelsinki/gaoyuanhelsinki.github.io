01 · ABOUT

Academic Profile

Gao Yuan received his doctoral training in the history of European philosophy at the University of Helsinki, under the supervision of Academician Simo Knuuttila and Professor Miikka Ruokanen, both prominent representatives of the Finnish scholarly tradition. His research has since developed at the intersection of European intellectual history, Nordic studies, the philosophy of emotions, Chinese philosophy, and comparative civilizational studies.

A central feature of his work is the attempt to connect intellectual history with area studies: examining how philosophical traditions travel across languages, religions, cultural regions, and political spaces. Particular attention is given to Finland and the wider Nordic region as a distinctive field of Sino-European intellectual encounter.
