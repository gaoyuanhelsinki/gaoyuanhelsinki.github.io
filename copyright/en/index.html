Gao Yuan Academic Website

This page sets out the principles governing original academic content, research introductions, academic citation, republication, and third-party materials on Gao Yuan’s academic website (gaoyuanhelsinki.cn). The website serves scholarly research, knowledge sharing, and the presentation of research findings while respecting the lawful rights and interests of authors and third-party rights holders.

01 · COPYRIGHT

Original Content & Copyright

This is the academic website of Gao Yuan (高源). Unless otherwise stated, copyright and related lawful rights in the original research introductions, accounts of research findings, thematic essays, academic views, original text and images, and other original written presentations created by Gao Yuan on this website belong to Gao Yuan.

Except as permitted by law or with authorization, original content from this website must not be copied in full, republished in its entirety, adapted, translated, compiled, redistributed, or otherwise used beyond the limits permitted by law. Please obtain prior permission from Gao Yuan for commercial use, substantial republication, or other uses beyond reasonable quotation.

02 · ACADEMIC CITATION

Academic Citation

Reasonable quotation of this website is welcome for scholarship, teaching, research, and non-commercial knowledge sharing, within the limits permitted by applicable law and academic standards. Citations should clearly identify the author, the title of the content or article, the website name, the original page URL, and the date accessed.

Suggested webpage citation
Gao, Yuan. “Title of the Webpage or Research Introduction.” Gao Yuan Academic Website. URL of the cited page. Accessed DD Month YYYY.
03 · PUBLISHED RESEARCH

Published Articles & Books

For research introduced on this website that has been formally published in academic journals, books, edited volumes, or other publications, scholarly citations should normally refer first to the complete bibliographic information of the original publication.

If an original online research guide, abstract, thematic overview, or research introduction provided on this website is cited separately, the relevant original webpage should also be acknowledged.

04 · THIRD-PARTY MATERIALS

Third-Party Materials

For the purposes of introducing academic research, documenting sources, and presenting research findings, this website may display or cite journal covers, publication details, documentary materials, institutional names, institutional webpages, and other third-party materials.

Copyright, trademark rights, and other related rights in these materials remain with their respective rights holders. Their reasonable display or quotation on this website does not constitute a claim to those rights.

05 · ATTRIBUTION

Authorship & Source Attribution

Quotation, republication, and other lawful uses of content from this website must retain the necessary attribution to the author, Gao Yuan (高源), and information identifying the original source. Such uses must not distort, take out of context, or materially alter the academic views or conclusions expressed in the original, or otherwise mislead readers about authorship, research conclusions, or the original source.

06 · REUSE & PERMISSION

Reuse & Permission

For full-text republication, substantial reproduction, commercial use, translation for publication, inclusion in a database, or other uses of original content beyond ordinary reasonable quotation, please contact Gao Yuan using the contact details provided on this website.

Important note: This policy clarifies the ownership of original content and standards for academic use. Quotation, commentary, teaching, and research uses permitted by law remain subject to applicable law and academic standards. Authors and sources must be accurately acknowledged.
